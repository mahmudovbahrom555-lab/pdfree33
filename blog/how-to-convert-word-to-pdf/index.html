Common reasons to convert Word to PDF

  • Job applications: Most job portals and recruiters expect a résumé and cover letter as PDF — it renders identically no matter what device or Word version opens it.
  • Signed contracts: Once a Word document is finalized or signed, converting to PDF locks the layout and makes accidental edits far less likely.
  • Academic and grant submissions: Universities and funding portals almost always require PDF, not .docx, for final submissions.
  • Cross-platform sharing: A .docx can reflow or substitute fonts on a different computer. A PDF looks exactly the same on Windows, Mac, mobile, or in a browser preview.
  • Archiving reports: PDF is the standard format for long-term, tamper-resistant record keeping.

Is it safe to convert a confidential Word document online?

Yes — when you use PDFree, and only because of how it's actually built, not just a promise. Your .docx file is parsed and rendered into a PDF entirely inside your browser using JavaScript and WebAssembly. No data is transmitted to any server at any point — not for upload, not for processing, not for the download. Contracts, résumés with personal details, financial reports, and legal documents never leave your device.

This is a structural guarantee, not a policy: PDFree has no server-side file-processing infrastructure to send your document to in the first place. Cloud-based converters like iLovePDF, Smallpdf, or Adobe's online tools upload a full copy of your file to their servers to do the conversion — meaning your document exists, even briefly, on hardware you don't control. For anything confidential, that's the difference that matters.

Frequently asked questions

Will the PDF text be selectable, or does it turn my document into an image?

The text stays real, selectable, and searchable — this isn't a screenshot. Paragraphs, headings, tables, and lists convert to genuine PDF text you can highlight, copy, and search.

Does it work with the older .doc format, or only .docx?

Currently only the modern .docx format (Word 2007 and later). If you have an older .doc file, re-save it as .docx in Word first, then convert.

Can I convert a Word document to PDF on my phone?

Yes. PDFree works in mobile browsers — Chrome for Android and Safari for iOS. Use the file picker to select your .docx and convert it directly on your phone.

What if I need to go the other way — PDF back to Word?

Use PDFree's PDF to Word tool — same privacy guarantee, converts a PDF into an editable .docx entirely in your browser.
