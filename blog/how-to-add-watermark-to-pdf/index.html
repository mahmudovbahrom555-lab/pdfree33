Common watermark use cases

CONFIDENTIAL

Legal documents, HR files, contracts, and financial reports that should not be shared outside the organization. A diagonal CONFIDENTIAL stamp at 30–40% opacity makes the status clear without obscuring the content.

DRAFT

Working versions of reports, proposals, or designs that are not yet final. Stamping DRAFT prevents the recipient from treating an in-progress document as the official version.

Company name or logo text

Brand your PDFs with your company name or website URL. Useful for distributing presentations, ebooks, or white papers where you want to maintain brand presence even if the file is shared onwards.

Copyright notice

Photographers, designers, and writers watermark PDFs with "© 2026 Your Name" to assert ownership on shared portfolios or sample work.

DO NOT COPY / FOR REVIEW ONLY

Exam papers, evaluation copies, and review materials where distribution should be restricted.

Watermark opacity guide

Opacity is the most important setting to get right. Too high and the watermark obscures the text beneath; too low and it's barely noticeable.

Opacity Best for
10–20% Subtle background marking — barely visible, just enough to identify the document
25–40% CONFIDENTIAL / DRAFT — clearly visible on page but doesn't block reading
50–70% Strong branding or copyright — prominent, harder to ignore
80–100% Proof / sample copies — intentionally obstructs content to prevent use

Frequently asked questions

How do I add a CONFIDENTIAL watermark?

Open pdfree.io/watermark-pdf/, load your PDF, type CONFIDENTIAL in the text field, set opacity to 30–40%, and click "Add Watermark". The stamped PDF downloads immediately — no upload required.

Can I watermark only specific pages?

PDFree applies the watermark to all pages at once. To watermark only specific pages: use Split PDF to separate the pages you want to stamp, apply the watermark, then combine everything back with Merge PDF.

Is the watermark permanent?

Yes. The watermark is embedded into the PDF page content, not as a layer that can be toggled off. For practical purposes it is permanent — a standard PDF viewer cannot remove it.

Does adding a watermark increase the file size?

Very slightly. A text watermark is stored as a PDF drawing operation — typically under 5 KB per page. For a 10-page PDF the size increase is negligible.

Can I add an image or logo watermark?

PDFree supports text watermarks only. Text covers the most common needs: CONFIDENTIAL, DRAFT, company name, copyright notice, DO NOT COPY. For image-based watermarks, desktop PDF software is required.
