The best free PDF tool for security and privacy

PDFree is designed to provide professional-grade PDF manipulation without the risks associated with cloud uploads. When you use our tools, the processing happens directly inside your web browser using WebAssembly. This means your sensitive documents, such as legal contracts, financial reports, or personal ID scans, are never transmitted over the internet.

We believe essential tools should be accessible to everyone. That's why PDFree has absolutely no file size limits, no daily usage caps, and no mandatory watermarks. It is a completely free, unlimited utility built for students, professionals, and anyone who values digital privacy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my files stored on your servers?
No. PDFree operates entirely on your device (client-side). We don't have servers that process your files, which guarantees 100% privacy.
Is there a limit to the file size or number of pages?
No, there are no artificial limits. The only limitation is the RAM (memory) available on your computer or smartphone.
