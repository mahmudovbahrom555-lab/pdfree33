Fill PDF Form Online Free

Fill out any PDF form directly in your browser — type names, dates, addresses, check boxes. No software to install, no account required, and your files never leave your device.

How to fill a PDF form online

  1. Upload your PDF form using the drop zone above.
  2. Your form fields are detected automatically — text fields, checkboxes, radio buttons, and dropdowns appear as a native HTML form.
  3. Fill in your information. On mobile, the right keyboard appears automatically (email, phone, date).
  4. Click Fill & Download PDF — your completed PDF saves to your device instantly.

Works with AcroForm PDF fields

PDFree detects AcroForm fields — the standard interactive form format used by Adobe Acrobat, government agencies, and HR departments. Text fields, checkboxes, radio buttons, dropdowns and list boxes are all supported. For scanned or flat PDFs without embedded form fields, a clear message is shown.

Your draft is automatically saved in your browser as you type, so you never lose progress if you accidentally close the tab.

Privacy — your forms stay private

PDF forms often contain sensitive personal information — SSNs, addresses, financial data. With PDFree, that data never leaves your browser. There are no servers processing your documents, no cloud storage, and nothing to breach. The entire tool runs as client-side JavaScript on your own device.

Frequently asked questions

Is filling PDF forms on PDFree really free?

Yes, completely free. No subscription, no premium tier, no per-file charges. The tool runs in your browser using open-source libraries.

Do I need to create an account?

No account required. Upload your PDF, fill it, download — no signup, no email, no login.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

Never. All processing happens inside your browser. Your PDF never leaves your device — no server ever receives it.

What types of PDF forms can I fill?

Any PDF with AcroForm fields — text fields, checkboxes, radio buttons, and dropdowns are detected automatically. Scanned or flat PDFs without form fields will show a message explaining the limitation.
