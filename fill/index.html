How to fill a PDF form online

  1. Open the PDFree annotation tool and upload your PDF form.
  2. Select the Text tool from the toolbar and click anywhere on the form to type your information.
  3. Use Check or Cross symbols to fill checkbox fields.
  4. Add your name, date, or any text in the appropriate fields.
  5. Download the completed PDF — ready to send or print.

Works with any PDF form

PDFree works with both interactive PDF forms (with clickable fields) and static/scanned PDFs (where fields are just printed lines). You overlay your text directly on top of the document, so the output always looks correct regardless of how the PDF was made.

Common use cases include job application forms, government documents, rental agreements, tax forms, medical intake forms, and any other document you receive as a PDF and need to complete.

Privacy — your forms stay private

PDF forms often contain sensitive personal information — SSNs, addresses, financial data. With PDFree, that data never leaves your browser. There are no servers processing your documents, no cloud storage, and nothing to breach. The entire tool runs as client-side JavaScript on your own device.
