PDF to JPG Converter — High Quality, Free, No Upload

Turn PDF pages into high-quality JPG or PNG images. Perfect for sharing individual pages on social media, creating thumbnails, or extracting graphics from documents. Choose your resolution (DPI) and format — all processing happens in your browser.

Resolution Options

  • 72 DPI: Web-optimized. Small file size, perfect for websites, social media, or email. Typical output: 800×1000 pixels per page.
  • 150 DPI: Balanced quality. Good for presentations, digital sharing, or light printing. Typical output: 1650×2100 pixels.
  • 300 DPI: Professional print quality. Large files, maximum detail for graphic design or high-resolution printing. Typical output: 3300×4200 pixels.

Format Choice: JPG or PNG

JPG (JPEG): Smaller files, slight compression. Best for photos, scanned documents, or pages with complex graphics. Loses transparency (white background added).

PNG: Larger files, lossless compression. Best for diagrams, charts, or text-heavy pages where crisp edges matter. Preserves transparency if present in original.

Use Cases

Social Media: Extract infographic pages, data visualization, or presentation slides to share on Twitter, LinkedIn, or Instagram. 72 DPI JPG is perfect for this.

Web Publishing: Convert report pages to images for blog posts or articles. Embed specific pages as screenshots without requiring PDF reader plugins.

Graphic Design: Extract vector diagrams or logos from PDF portfolios at 300 DPI for use in other design software.

Archival: Convert important document pages to image format for redundant backup storage. Images are universally viewable without specialized software.

How It Works

PDFree uses pdf.js (the same rendering engine powering Firefox's built-in PDF viewer) to draw each page onto an HTML5 Canvas. The canvas is then converted to JPG or PNG image data using the browser's native encoding.

This approach produces pixel-perfect renderings identical to what you see when viewing the PDF normally. Text remains sharp, colors are preserved accurately, and embedded images are extracted at their full resolution (up to your chosen DPI limit).

⚡ Processing Time: Large PDFs (50+ pages) at 300 DPI take longer to convert. Each page must be rendered individually. Expect 1-3 seconds per page at high resolution.

Privacy Note

The entire conversion happens in your browser. Your PDF is loaded into memory, rendered to canvas, and converted to image format — all without any server communication. The images are generated locally and downloaded directly from your browser's RAM.
