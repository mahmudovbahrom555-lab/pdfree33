Claim files routinely contain some of the most sensitive data a claimant has:

Medical records & diagnoses
Bank & payment details

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe to process claimant medical and financial data with a free online tool?

With PDFree, yes. No file is transmitted to any server. Medical records, bank details, and social security numbers stay in your browser's memory and are cleared when you close the tab. Most other free PDF tools upload your document — check their privacy policy before processing claimant data with them.

Can I batch compress dozens of claim attachments at once?

Yes. Select all the scanned attachments at once in the Compress PDF tool — each is compressed separately, and you get a ZIP with every file shrunk down, still individual. See the full batch processing guide for all supported tools.

Does PDFree work offline — can an adjuster process files in the field without an internet connection?

Yes. Once the page has loaded at least once, PDFree functions as a Progressive Web App and works fully offline. This is useful for adjusters processing sensitive claim documents on a device without a reliable connection.
