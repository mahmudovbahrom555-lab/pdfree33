Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe to process employee personal data with a free online tool?

With PDFree, yes. No file is transmitted to any server. Social security numbers, salary details, and medical information stay in your browser's memory and are cleared when you close the tab. Most other free PDF tools upload your document — check their privacy policy before processing employee data with them.

Can I batch password-protect a whole onboarding cohort at once?

Yes. Select all the onboarding packets at once in the Protect PDF tool — each is encrypted separately with the same password policy, and you get a ZIP with every protected file, still individual. See the full batch processing guide for all supported tools.

Does PDFree work offline — can I process files without an internet connection?

Yes. Once the page has loaded at least once, PDFree functions as a Progressive Web App and works fully offline. This is useful for processing sensitive HR documents on a device not connected to the internet.
