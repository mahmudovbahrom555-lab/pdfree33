Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe to process student assignments with a free online tool?

With PDFree, student data is never uploaded to any server. All processing happens in your browser's memory and disappears when you close the tab. This is different from most free PDF tools which upload files to remote servers. Whether this meets your school or district's specific data handling requirements depends on your local policies — consult your IT department if unsure.

Can I merge 30 student worksheets into one class PDF?

Yes. PDFree Merge has no file count limit. Drop all 30 worksheet files at once, arrange them in the order you want, and click Merge. The single combined PDF downloads directly to your device in seconds.

Does OCR work for non-English student work?

PDFree OCR supports many languages. PDF to Word text mode handles Arabic, Hebrew (right-to-left), Cyrillic (Russian, Ukrainian), CJK (Chinese, Japanese, Korean), and Devanagari (Hindi) — scripts that most free tools handle poorly. For best OCR results, the scan should be clear and high-contrast.
