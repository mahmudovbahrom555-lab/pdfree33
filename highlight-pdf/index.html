Highlight PDF Online — Mark Important Text and Areas

Add colored highlights to any PDF directly in your browser. Mark key passages, annotate study materials, or flag contract clauses — with full color and opacity control. Your files are never uploaded anywhere.

How to Highlight a PDF

  1. Drop your PDF onto the page or click "Choose file" — the PDF renders locally, nothing is uploaded
  2. Select your highlight color and adjust opacity to taste
  3. Click and drag over text or areas to apply the highlight
  4. Repeat across any number of pages, then download your highlighted PDF

When to Highlight a PDF

Studying & Research: Mark key passages in textbooks, papers, or lecture notes so you can review them later without re-reading the whole document.

Contract Review: Highlight important clauses, deadlines, or obligations before signing or discussing with colleagues.

Document Feedback: Flag sections that need revision, require a second opinion, or contain data to extract — without altering the original text.

Presentations: Call attention to key figures or quotes before sharing a PDF with an audience.

Is It Safe to Highlight PDFs Online?

Yes. PDFree processes all highlighting entirely in your browser using pdf-lib. Your PDF is never uploaded to any server — no one sees your document or what you choose to mark. This makes PDFree safe for confidential contracts, medical records, and personal files.
