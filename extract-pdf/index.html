Extract Pages from PDF Online — Free, Private, No Upload

Need to extract specific pages from a PDF? PDFree's split tool lets you select exactly which pages to keep, then download them as separate files or as a single PDF. Everything happens in your browser — no uploads, no servers, completely private.

Two Ways to Split

Separate Files: Each selected page becomes its own PDF. Perfect for splitting a multi-page scan into individual documents.

Single File: All selected pages are combined into one new PDF. Useful for extracting a specific section (like pages 5-12) from a longer document.

Quick Selection Tools

  • Odd Pages: Extract pages 1, 3, 5, 7... — useful for scanned documents where odd pages are one-sided
  • Even Pages: Extract pages 2, 4, 6, 8...
  • First Half / Second Half: Split a document down the middle
  • Select All / Deselect All: Batch operations for large PDFs

Use Cases

Legal & Compliance: Extract specific exhibits or sections from contracts for separate distribution.

Academic: Pull out individual chapters from textbooks or specific pages from research papers.

Administrative: Separate invoice pages or split multi-employee payroll PDFs into individual files.

⚡ Privacy Note: Your PDF never leaves your device. The page thumbnails you see are rendered in your browser, not on a server.

Related Tools

  • Merge PDF — Combine the extracted pages with other documents
  • Split PDF — Split the whole document into individual pages
  • Rotate PDF — Fix orientation of extracted pages
