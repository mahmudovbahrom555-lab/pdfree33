Why sign with PDFree instead of DocuSign or Adobe?

Most e-signature platforms require creating an account and uploading your document to their servers. For sensitive contracts — NDAs, employment agreements, financial documents — that means your private document is stored on a third-party server, subject to their privacy policy and potential data breaches.

PDFree processes everything locally in your browser. Your document never leaves your device. There is no account to create, no subscription to manage, and no usage limits.

Is a typed signature legally valid?

In most jurisdictions, yes. A typed name on a document constitutes a valid electronic signature under the US ESIGN Act, UETA, and EU eIDAS for the majority of business agreements — employment contracts, service agreements, consent forms, and more.

For documents that legally require a qualified electronic signature (QES) with a cryptographic certificate — such as certain government filings or notarized documents — you would need a specialized certified e-signature service. For everyday business use, a typed signature is sufficient.
