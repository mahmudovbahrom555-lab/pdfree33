Why PDFree Needs No Signup

Most PDF tools require an account because they store your files on their servers. An account lets them associate your files with you, enforce plan limits, and send you marketing emails. PDFree needs none of this because it processes files entirely in your browser — there are no files to store, no limits to enforce, and no personal data to track.

The only reason you'd ever interact with PDFree's infrastructure is to load the app code — and that happens via a CDN like any other website. After your first visit, even that can happen from cache.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I really use PDFree without creating an account?

Yes, completely. PDFree has no login system, no user accounts, and no registration page. Open any tool in your browser and use it immediately. There is nothing to sign up for, ever.

Why don't I need to sign up?

Accounts exist to store files on a server or enforce usage limits. PDFree doesn't store files (everything is local in your browser) and has no limits. There's simply no function an account would serve.

Do I need to give my email address?

No. PDFree never asks for your email address, phone number, or any personal information. There are no newsletters, no confirmation emails, and no marketing communications of any kind.

Is there a trial period or hidden paid tier?

No. PDFree is not a trial. There is no paid plan to upgrade to, no features locked behind a paywall, and no credit card required at any point. All 16+ tools are free forever.

What's the catch?

The project is open source (GNU AGPLv3) and runs at minimal cost because there are no servers processing your files — your browser does all the work. Plausible Analytics (privacy-friendly, no cookies, GDPR-compliant) measures basic traffic stats. That's the full picture.
