Why People Look for an iLovePDF Alternative

iLovePDF is a useful tool, but it comes with real limitations that push users to look for alternatives:

  • Files are uploaded to their servers — every document you process goes to iLovePDF's infrastructure, creating a potential privacy and security exposure window
  • Daily task limits on free plan — hit the limit and you're blocked until the next day or forced to upgrade
  • File size restrictions — large PDFs are rejected or require a paid plan
  • Slow on large files — upload time + server processing + download adds latency
  • Not suitable for sensitive documents — contracts, medical records, and financial data shouldn't be uploaded to third-party servers

Frequently Asked Questions

Does iLovePDF upload files to a server?

Yes. iLovePDF uploads your files to their servers for processing and stores them for up to 2 hours. PDFree is the opposite — all processing is local in your browser, your files never leave your device.

Is PDFree really completely free with no limits?

Yes. PDFree has no daily limits, no file size caps, no watermarks, no account requirement, and no paid tier. All tools are free forever. The project is open source under GNU AGPLv3.

What does PDFree do that iLovePDF doesn't?

PDFree processes files entirely locally — no upload, no server, no internet required after first load. This makes it safe for confidential documents and usable offline. iLovePDF cannot offer either of these because it is server-dependent by design.

Does PDFree work on mobile?

Yes. PDFree works on iOS Safari, Android Chrome, and all modern mobile browsers. You can also install it as a PWA from your browser's "Add to Home Screen" option for quick access.
