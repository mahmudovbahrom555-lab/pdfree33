    Draw on PDF Free — Add Arrows, Text & Shapes

    Annotate any PDF directly in your browser. Add arrows to point out important details, draw shapes, write text notes, or erase mistakes. Your files never leave your device — everything happens locally.

    Why use PDFree Draw?

    • Private by design — processing happens in your browser, nothing is uploaded
    • Arrows & shapes — perfect for creating visual instructions or marking up documents
    • Free forever — no account, no watermarks, no limits
    • Multi-page — annotate every page, then download as a single PDF

    How to draw on a PDF

    1. Drop your PDF onto the page or click "Choose file"
    2. Select a tool — pen, arrow, line, rectangle, oval, text or eraser
    3. Draw on the page. Use Undo / Redo as needed
    4. Navigate between pages with the arrows below the canvas
    5. Click "Download Annotated PDF" to save your work
