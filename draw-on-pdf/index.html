    Draw on PDF Free — Add Arrows, Text & Shapes

    Annotate any PDF directly in your browser. Add arrows to point out important details, draw shapes, write text notes, or erase mistakes. Your files never leave your device — everything happens locally.

    Why use PDFree Draw?

    • Private by design — processing happens in your browser, nothing is uploaded
    • Arrows & shapes — perfect for creating visual instructions or marking up documents
    • Free forever — no account, no watermarks, no limits
    • Multi-page — annotate every page, then download as a single PDF

    How to draw on a PDF

    1
    Drop your PDF onto the page or click "Choose file"

    The PDF renders page by page in the canvas editor — nothing is uploaded to any server.

    2
    Select a drawing tool from the toolbar

    Choose from pen, arrow, line, rectangle, oval, highlight, text or eraser. Each tool has adjustable colour and size.

    3
    Draw on the page — use Undo / Redo freely

    All annotations are non-destructive until you save. Navigate between pages with the arrows below the canvas.

    4
    Click "Download Annotated PDF" to save your work

    Annotations are flattened into the PDF and downloaded instantly. The original file on your device is unchanged.
