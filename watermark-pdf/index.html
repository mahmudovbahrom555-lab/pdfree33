Add Watermark to PDF Online — Text & Image, Free

Protect your documents from unauthorized use or distribution by adding a custom text watermark. PDFree lets you choose the text, position, color, and opacity — all processed in your browser with complete privacy.

Watermark Options

  • Custom Text: Add any message — "CONFIDENTIAL", "DRAFT", your company name, or copyright notice
  • Position Control: Center, top, bottom, or tile across every page
  • Color Choice: Gray (subtle), red (urgent), or blue (professional)
  • Adjustable Opacity: From barely visible (10%) to fully opaque (100%)
  • Real-Time Preview: See exactly how your watermark will look before applying

Common Use Cases

Legal & Compliance: Mark contract drafts as "NOT FOR EXECUTION" or "COPY" to prevent confusion with originals. Watermark NDAs with "CONFIDENTIAL" to remind recipients of their obligations.

Academic: Protect research papers or thesis drafts with "DRAFT" watermarks before final submission. Mark student work with class identifiers or submission dates.

Business: Add company branding to proposals, reports, and presentations. Mark invoices with "PAID" or "VOID" after processing. Watermark internal documents with department names or project codes.

Creative: Copyright protect photography portfolios, design mockups, or illustration samples shared with clients. Add "PROOF" watermarks to prevent unauthorized use.

Technical Implementation

PDFree overlays watermark text directly onto PDF page content using pdf-lib's drawing API. The watermark becomes part of the document structure — it's not a separate layer that can be easily removed.

Unlike image-based watermarks that degrade quality, text watermarks remain crisp at any zoom level. The watermark is rendered using vector graphics, so printing or viewing at high resolution maintains perfect clarity.

⚡ Pro Tip: For "DRAFT" watermarks, use 30-40% opacity in gray. For "CONFIDENTIAL" markings, use 20% opacity in red to create urgency without obscuring text.

Privacy & Security

Your PDF never leaves your browser. The watermark is applied locally in JavaScript — no server sees your document content. This is critical for confidential materials where even encrypted transmission to a server poses risk.
