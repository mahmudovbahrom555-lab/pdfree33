Quick Edit PDF — Fix a Typo Without Leaving Your Browser

Need to fix a typo, a date, or a name inside a PDF — without opening Word, without a subscription, without uploading the file anywhere? Quick Edit PDF opens your PDF's own pages, lets you tap any line and retype it, then replaces just that line's text in the original file — every other page, picture and line stays as it was. All on your own device.

Built for small text fixes, not a full rewrite: retype a word, a line, a paragraph fragment. Adding or removing whole paragraphs, restructuring tables, or reflowing layout isn't supported — see the FAQ below for exactly what this tool is and isn't for. Last verified: November 2026.

How to Edit Text in a PDF in 3 Steps

1
Open your PDF

Click Choose file or drag your PDF into the drop zone. Its pages open as they are.

2
Click the text you want to fix

Your PDF's pages open as they are. Tap any line, retype it, and press Enter; the new text must fit the space of that line. Use the page arrows to reach any page, and the zoom button on a phone.

3
Save and download

Click Save. PDFree removes the old text of each edited line, writes the new text in its place, reads every changed page back to check it, and the PDF downloads.

What Quick Edit PDF Is (and Isn't) For

Good for

Fixing a typo, correcting a date or a name, tweaking a sentence, updating a number in a table cell — small, targeted text changes anywhere in the document, including headings, lists, and table cells.

Not built for

Adding or deleting whole paragraphs, inserting new table rows, changing page layout, or reflowing text — this tool edits existing text in place, it doesn't restructure a document. For that level of editing, convert to Word first (see PDF to Word below), edit fully there, then convert back.

When a file can't be edited in place

A scan has no text to change, and fillable forms and signed PDFs aren't edited in place. Arabic, Persian and Hebrew text isn't edited in place yet — such files open in the previous editor. For a file with text, Use the previous editor rebuilds the document as editable text instead; before that editor opens, PDFree scores how cleanly the structure converted and, if it's too damaged, points you to PDF to Word rather than risk a PDF built on broken structure.

Why Edit Here Instead of a Cloud Tool?

Most online PDF editors upload your file to a remote server to make it editable, then send back a result. A contract, a signed form, an ID scan, an internal memo — whatever is in the PDF briefly exists on hardware you don't control, and some services keep a copy.

PDFree works differently. Your PDF is read and edited entirely inside your own browser. No server receives it — not even PDFree's own servers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my PDF uploaded anywhere to edit it?

No. The whole process — opening the PDF, replacing the edited lines and checking the result — runs entirely in your browser. Your document never leaves your device.

Can I add new paragraphs or delete a table row?

Not with this tool — Quick Edit PDF is built for retyping existing text in place, not restructuring a document. If you need to add or remove content, convert to Word with PDF to Word, make the full edit there, then convert back to PDF.

Why did it say my PDF can't be edited here?

A file with text opens on its own pages, where you edit it in place. A scan has no text to change; fillable forms and signed PDFs aren't edited in place. The previous editor, which rebuilds the document as editable text, first checks how cleanly your PDF's structure converts — scanned documents, unusual layouts or damaged text extraction can score too low to edit safely, and then it suggests the full PDF to Word converter instead.

Does editing change the formatting of the rest of the document?

No. Only the text of the lines you retype changes: their old text is removed and the new text is written in the same place. Pictures, other lines and other pages stay as they were, and every changed page is read back before you download it.

Does PDFree store or log my files after processing?

No. PDFree runs entirely in your browser — there is no backend server that receives your files. Because nothing is uploaded, nothing can be stored, logged, or retained. Your document exists only in your browser's memory for the duration of the session and is cleared when you close the tab.

Is this a free PDF editor with no sign up?

Yes. PDFree is completely free — no sign up, no account, no installation. Open the tool, load your PDF, click the text you want to fix, and download the result.

Can I find and replace text on every page?

Yes. Type the text in Find and what should stand there in Replace with — leave it empty to remove the text, such as a line repeated at the foot of every page. PDFree shows how many it found and on how many pages, and Replace all changes them in the original PDF. A match has to sit within one line, and the new text has to fit the space of its line; a line that can't be changed in place is left as it was, and its page is named.

Can I create one PDF per recipient from a list of names?

Yes. Choose Personalized PDF copies in Quick Edit PDF before opening a file. Paste the names or import an Excel or CSV list, mark where the name goes, then download the PDFs as a ZIP. To add a watermark as well, turn on Also add a watermark to each copy: write {name} in its text, such as Copy for {name}, and each copy gets its recipient's name there too.
