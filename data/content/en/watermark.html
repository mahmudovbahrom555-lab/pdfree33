What does opacity control?

Opacity of 100% makes the watermark fully opaque (solid, dark). Opacity of 20–30% creates the classic 'ghost' watermark that is visible but doesn't obscure the content underneath. Most use cases work well at 20–40%.

Can I set the watermark color?

Yes. You can set any hex color or choose from common presets. Light grey (#CCCCCC at 30% opacity) gives a professional, unobtrusive result.

What is the difference between a watermark and a stamp on a PDF?

The terms are often used interchangeably. A watermark is typically a semi-transparent text or image that repeats diagonally across every page (e.g. DRAFT at 30% opacity). A stamp is usually an opaque label placed once in a fixed position (e.g. APPROVED in a corner). PDFree lets you achieve either look by adjusting the opacity and position of the text watermark.
