Frequently Asked Questions

What is the page range format?

Enter pages and ranges separated by commas: 1, 3-7, 12, 15-20. Spaces are optional. Ranges are inclusive — 3-7 extracts pages 3, 4, 5, 6, and 7.

What is the difference between Extract and Split?

Split has richer selection options (odd pages, even pages, halves, visual thumbnail selection) and can produce multiple separate files. Extract is simpler — enter a range, get one PDF. Use whichever fits your workflow.

Will the extracted pages preserve formatting exactly?

Yes. Pages are copied at the PDF object level — fonts, images, links, and formatting are identical to the originals.

Can I extract pages from a password-protected PDF?

No. Encrypted PDFs cannot be modified without the password. Remove the password first, then extract the pages.

Is there a limit on how many pages I can extract?

No. You can extract any number of pages from a PDF of any size, as long as your browser has enough memory to hold the file.

How do I extract a single page from a PDF?

Type the page number in the range field — for example 5 to extract only page 5. The result is a new PDF containing just that one page. All formatting, images, and links are preserved exactly as they appear in the original.

Can I extract pages from a scanned PDF?

Yes. Scanned PDFs are images embedded in a PDF container — extraction works identically to any other PDF. The pages are copied at the object level, so the scan quality is completely unchanged in the output file.

Does extracting pages change the original file?

No. PDFree reads your file in your browser's memory and creates a new PDF with the selected pages. The original file on your device is never modified — it stays exactly as it was.
