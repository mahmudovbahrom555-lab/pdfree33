Why Clean Scan Handles Uneven Lighting, Not Just a Global Filter

Many quick fixes for a grey scan amount to a single global brightness or contrast slider — fine when a page is evenly lit, and a muddy or blown-out mess when it isn't. Real scanned pages, especially from books, are rarely evenly lit: the gutter near the spine is darker, the outer edge is lighter, and a flat adjustment either leaves the dark side grey or blows the light side out to nothing. Clean Scan estimates each page's own lighting pattern first, then flattens it — so a page that's dark on one side and light on the other comes out uniformly white on both sides, with the text preserved in between.

When You Need to Clean a Scan

Scanned books and bound documents

The curve of a book's spine casts a shadow that a flatbed scanner can't avoid. Clean Scan flattens that shadow instead of leaving half the page grey.

Old or yellowed paper

Aged documents scan with a yellow or beige cast baked into the image. Whiten the background while keeping faded text legible.

Photocopies of photocopies

Each generation of copying adds noise and grey. Clean it up before archiving, printing, or sharing a document that's been copied one too many times.

Preparing scans for OCR or long-term archiving

A clean, high-contrast background makes text recognition more reliable and produces a more professional-looking archival copy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does cleaning a scan upload my file to a server?

No. Every page is analyzed and rebuilt entirely in your browser. Your file is never sent anywhere.

What's the difference between Clean and Enhance mode?

Clean flattens the background to pure white and darkens text for maximum contrast — best for plain text pages. Enhance brightens the page and boosts contrast without fully whitening the background, which keeps stamps, signatures, and photos on the page recognizable.

Will this remove color photos or stamps from my document?

Clean mode can fade or remove color content, since it's designed to push everything toward black text on a white background. If a page contains color you want to keep, use Enhance mode instead — the tool warns you automatically when it detects color on the page you're previewing.

Why does my scan have a shadow or grey gradient in the first place?

Flatbed scanners and phone cameras rarely light a page perfectly evenly — a book's spine casts a shadow near the gutter, ambient light falls off toward the edges, and aged paper reflects light unevenly. That unevenness gets captured as part of the image itself, which is exactly what Clean Scan's background analysis is built to correct.

Is there a limit on how many pages I can clean?

No. There's no page-count or file-size limit, and no per-hour task limit. Clean a single-page scan or a hundred-page document as often as you need.

Does cleaning reduce image quality?

Clean mode saves pages as PNG, which avoids the compression artifacts that would blur sharp black-and-white text. Enhance mode saves as high-quality JPEG, appropriate for pages that still contain photos or continuous-tone content.

Can I use this on a regular PDF that isn't a scan?

You can, but it isn't the intended use — cleaning converts each page into an image, so real selectable text in a normal digital PDF would become a picture of text. The tool detects when a PDF looks like it has real text content and shows a warning before you process it.

Can I clean a scan on my phone?

Yes. PDFree works in mobile Safari (iOS) and Chrome for Android — the same mode, strength, and preview controls work with a tap.

Is the Clean Scan tool completely free?

Yes. PDFree is free with no subscription, no daily or hourly limits, and no signup. No watermark is added to the output and there's no file size restriction.
