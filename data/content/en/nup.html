Pages per Sheet — Put Multiple PDF Pages on One Page

Print a PDF with 2, 4, 6, 9 or 16 pages on each sheet of paper, turn it into a booklet to fold and staple, or put invoices two or four to an A4 page at their real size for an expense report. PDFree lays the pages out in your browser and keeps them as vector content: text stays sharp, selectable and searchable, nothing is turned into a picture.

Several PDFs can go in at once — their pages follow one another in the order of the list. Last verified: October 2026.

How to Put Several Pages on One Sheet

1
Add your PDF files

Click Choose files or drop one or more PDFs. A preview shows the first sheet with every page number in its place.

2
Pick pages per sheet

2, 4, 6, 9 or 16 — or the Booklet or Invoices on A4 mode. Paper size (A4, A3, Letter) and orientation are chosen for you so the pages come out as large as possible — you can still set them yourself.

3
Download the new PDF

Click Arrange Pages. The PDF downloads immediately, ready to print.

What It Is Used For

Saving paper

Two slides, two book pages or four handout pages on one sheet: half or a quarter of the paper, still readable.

Invoices for an expense report

The Invoices on A4 mode keeps each invoice or receipt at its real size and puts two or four on an A4 sheet. An optional 30 mm binding margin on the left leaves room for punching or gluing.

A booklet you fold yourself

The Booklet mode puts the pages in booklet order, two on each side of the sheet. Print double-sided, flipping on the short edge, fold the stack in the middle and staple it — a zine, a programme or a small manual reads 1, 2, 3… Blank pages are added at the end when the count is not a multiple of four.

Documents read right to left

For Arabic, Persian or Hebrew documents, Right to left puts page 1 in the top-right cell, the way those pages are read. It is switched on by itself on our Arabic pages.

Pages of different sizes

Every page keeps its proportions and is centred in its cell — A4 and Letter, portrait and landscape can be mixed. Pages rotated in the PDF are placed the way they are shown.

Why Do It Here Instead of a Cloud Tool?

Most online tools upload your file to a server, rearrange it there and send it back. Invoices, contracts and reports then pass through hardware you don't control.

PDFree does the whole layout inside your browser. The file is never uploaded — not even to PDFree — and there is no daily limit, no account and no watermark.
