Why Flatten a PDF?

Lock a filled form before submitting

Once you have filled in your tax form, government application or contract, flattening ensures the recipient cannot alter the values. The form data becomes part of the page — uneditable and permanent.

Ensure consistent display across viewers

Interactive form fields sometimes render differently in various PDF readers. A flattened PDF looks identical in Adobe Acrobat, macOS Preview, Chrome's PDF viewer, and mobile apps — because it is just static content.

Prepare PDFs for printing or archiving

Some print services and archiving systems expect flat PDFs without interactive layers. Flattening removes all form metadata, widgets and JavaScript — leaving a clean, universally compatible document.

Remove hidden field data

PDF form fields store their values as structured metadata. If you share a filled form without flattening, a recipient could inspect the raw PDF and see field names, original values, or metadata attached to the form object. Flattening removes all of that.

100% Private — Nothing Uploaded

Every step of the flatten operation runs inside your browser using pdf-lib, a battle-tested open-source library. Your PDF — with all the sensitive form data it contains — never reaches any server. Not PDFree's servers, not any third-party service.

This is especially important for forms containing personal or financial data: tax returns, insurance claims, medical records, contracts. You can flatten these with confidence that the data stays on your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does flattening a PDF actually do?

Flattening merges all interactive layers — form fields, annotations, digital signatures, and overlays — into the static page content. The result is a flat, non-interactive PDF that looks identical to the original but cannot be edited.

Can I reverse a flattened PDF?

No. Flattening is irreversible — the original form fields are permanently replaced by static visual content. Always keep a backup of your original filled PDF before flattening if you think you might need to change values later.

Does flattening remove digital signatures?

Drawn signatures created with the Fill PDF tool (signature pad images) are flattened into the page. Cryptographic digital signatures from certified e-signature providers may be handled differently — the visual appearance is preserved but the cryptographic certificate may no longer validate after flattening.

Is my PDF uploaded to any server?

No. Everything runs in your browser using pdf-lib. Neither your original PDF nor the flattened result is ever sent anywhere. Your document and its contents stay on your device at all times.
