Frequently Asked Questions

How many PDF files can I merge at once?

There is no limit. PDFree processes everything in your browser's memory, so you can merge 2, 50, or 100+ files in a single operation. For large batches (50+ files or 500MB+ total), close other browser tabs to free up RAM. The tool was built by someone who needed to merge 83 PDFs at once — that's exactly the use case it's designed for.

Will the formatting and fonts be preserved after merging?

Yes. PDFree uses pdf-lib, which copies pages at the PDF object level — fonts, images, vector graphics, and formatting are preserved exactly as in the originals.

Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

No. Encrypted PDFs cannot be read or modified without the password. Remove the password first using a PDF tool, then merge.

Is there a daily limit on how many merges I can do?

No. PDFree has no usage caps. Merge as many PDFs as you want, as often as you need. There is no daily limit, no task limit, and no file count limit.

Does merging upload my files to a server?

No. Your files are processed entirely in your browser's memory. Nothing is sent to any server. Close the tab and the files are gone from memory — no trace on any server.

Can I merge PDFs on a phone or tablet?

Yes. PDFree works in mobile browsers (Chrome for Android, Safari for iOS). Drag and drop is replaced by a file picker, and reordering works by tapping the up/down buttons.
