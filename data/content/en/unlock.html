You must know the correct password. PDFree cannot open a PDF without it — this is a password removal tool, not a recovery or cracking tool. If you don't know the password, you'll need to get it from whoever protected the file.

Why Unlock Here Instead of a Cloud Tool?

Most online PDF unlockers upload both your document and its password to a remote server. Even for a file you're only removing protection from, that's your password and your content briefly sitting on hardware you don't control.

PDFree works differently. The password never leaves your browser — it's used locally by QPDF, an open-source PDF library compiled to WebAssembly, which decrypts the file entirely on your device. No server receives your file or your password — not even PDFree's own servers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my PDF or password uploaded anywhere?

No. Password removal happens entirely in your browser using QPDF (WebAssembly). Neither your file nor your password is sent to any server.

Does PDFree store or log my files after processing?

No. PDFree runs entirely in your browser — there is no backend server that receives your files. Because nothing is uploaded, nothing can be stored, logged, or retained. Your document exists only in your browser's memory for the duration of the session and is cleared when you close the tab.

Can this recover or crack a password I don't know?

No. You must know the PDF's correct password. PDFree removes protection from files you're already authorized to open — it does not guess, recover, or bypass unknown passwords.

What if my PDF says "no password needed"?

Some PDFs only have owner-level restrictions (like disabled printing or copying) with no password required to open them. PDFree detects this automatically and lets you remove those restrictions without typing anything.

I entered the password but it says incorrect. What now?

Double-check for typos, extra spaces, or caps lock. Passwords are case-sensitive. Your file stays selected, so you can simply try again.

Is this a free PDF password remover with no sign up?

Yes. PDFree is completely free — no sign up, no account, no installation. Open the tool, enter the password, and download the unlocked PDF.

Can I re-add a password later?

Yes — use Protect PDF to add a new password or restrictions to the unlocked file.
