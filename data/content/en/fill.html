What PDF Forms Can You Fill?

Government and tax forms

IRS W-9, W-4, I-9, and most government AcroForms are fully supported. All interactive fields are detected and rendered as fillable inputs.

Medical and insurance forms

Patient intake forms, HIPAA authorization forms, and insurance claims contain sensitive health data. Processing them locally means that information never reaches a third-party server.

HR and employment forms

Onboarding paperwork, direct deposit authorizations, and non-disclosure agreements often require a signature. Use the built-in signature pad to sign without printing.

Contracts and agreements

Service agreements, rental contracts, and consent forms with AcroForm fields can be filled and signed in one step. The resulting PDF is a standard document that opens in Adobe Reader, Preview, and any other viewer.

Why Fill PDF Forms Here Instead of a Cloud Tool?

Most online PDF fillers upload your document to a remote server, process it there, and return the result. That means a copy of your form — with all the data you filled in — is transmitted over the internet and temporarily stored on someone else's hardware.

PDFree works differently. The entire filling operation runs inside your browser using pdf-lib and pdf.js, two battle-tested open-source libraries. Your PDF and everything you type into it never leave your device. No server receives your data, not even PDFree's own servers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my filled PDF uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser using pdf-lib and pdf.js. Neither your original PDF nor the filled version is ever sent to a server. PDFree does not have access to your document.

What if my PDF has no fillable fields?

PDFree detects AcroForm interactive fields. If the PDF is a scanned image or a flat document without embedded form fields, you will see a "No fillable fields found" message. For flat PDFs you can use the Annotate or Watermark tools to add text overlays.

Is the drawn signature legally binding?

A drawn signature image inserted into a PDF is an electronic signature. Its legal validity depends on jurisdiction and context — for many everyday documents (consent forms, internal approvals, rental agreements) it is sufficient. For regulated legal contexts, consider a certified e-signature service that provides an audit trail.

Can I fill a password-protected PDF?

Only if you have the owner password. Encrypted PDFs with edit restrictions cannot be filled without the credentials. Remove the password first using the Protect PDF tool (enter the password, then save with no new password).
