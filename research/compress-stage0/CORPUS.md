Record the SHA-256 from corpus.csv with every run. Keep the existing cached arXiv, Wikimedia, and archive.org revisions when a source URL now serves different bytes. Rights for cached files come from the repository's existing manifest; public downloads are not a blanket license for individually credited third-party assets. Do not redistribute source PDFs or transformed outputs without checking the relevant rights.

Treat CMYK vector controls separately from real photo-rich documents. In the raster column, ICC(4) denotes four-component ICC raster images as reported by Poppler; this is not an ICC profile or print conformance validation. No commercial print catalog with clearly reusable rights was established here. USGS photo-rich print publications with detected CMYK and the explicitly reusable CMYK controls cover the main color cases, with this limitation recorded.

Freeze the list before comparing results. Report success, failures, skipped inputs, input/output bytes, and quality per file, with separate summaries for forms, scans, photos and print. Do not discard files that do not shrink. Record page content streams without a Filter separately from image streams before evaluating stage 1. Size and SHA-256 belong to the inspected bytes, not to the source URL indefinitely.
