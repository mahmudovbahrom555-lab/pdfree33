FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do PDFree tools upload my files?
No. Every tool on PDFree runs entirely in your browser using WebAssembly. Your files are never uploaded to any server — they never leave your device. Zero cloud, zero uploads.
Is PDFree really free — no catch?
Yes. All 18 tools are free with no file size limits, no watermarks, no daily limits, and no account required. No credit card, no subscription. Free forever.
What PDF tools does PDFree offer?
PDFree offers merge, split, compress, rotate, protect, redact, watermark, add page numbers, edit metadata, JPG to PDF, PDF to JPG, extract pages, draw on PDF, sign PDF, fill PDF forms, highlight, annotate, and flatten — all free and private.
Does PDFree work on mobile?
Yes. PDFree is a Progressive Web App (PWA) that works in any modern browser on desktop, tablet, and mobile — including iOS Safari and Android Chrome. You can even install it for offline use.
Is PDFree safe for confidential documents?
Yes. Since files never leave your device, PDFree is safe for confidential documents, medical records, financial statements, and legal files. Processing happens in a sandboxed WebAssembly thread — nothing is logged, stored, or transmitted.
