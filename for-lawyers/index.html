Frequently Asked Questions

Can I watermark or password-protect an entire discovery set at once?

Yes. Batch mode on Watermark and Protect applies the same setting to every exhibit you select, keeping each file separate and returning them together in one ZIP. See the full batch processing guide for all supported tools.

Does using PDFree risk breaching attorney-client privilege?

Local processing helps reduce exposure because no file is transmitted to any third-party server — the document remains on your device throughout. Whether this fully satisfies your privilege obligations depends on your jurisdiction and internal firm policies. What we can say with certainty: PDFree does not receive, store, or process your files on any server. Consult your bar association guidelines for specific advice.

Is PDFree redaction permanent?

Yes. The Redact PDF tool overlays an opaque filled rectangle on the PDF content. The redacted PDF is reconstructed with the content permanently hidden — not just visually covered. The text underneath is not recoverable from the output file by selecting, copying, or examining the document structure.

How does local processing relate to GDPR obligations?

Since no file data is transmitted to PDFree's servers or any third party, local processing avoids many of the data-transfer obligations that arise when using cloud-based tools. Whether this fully satisfies your firm's GDPR compliance requirements depends on your context and internal policies. Consult your DPO for specific guidance.
