Annotation tools available

  • Text — type labels, names, dates, or any custom text anywhere on the page
  • Arrow — draw directional arrows to point at specific content
  • Rectangle — highlight or frame areas of the document
  • Check mark ✓ — mark completed items or correct answers
  • Cross ✗ — mark incorrect items or select "No" checkboxes
  • Plus + and Minus − — add visual indicators to content

Designed for mobile form filling

Filling and annotating PDFs on a phone is normally frustrating — fields are tiny and easy to miss. PDFree includes a zoom slider and a magnifier lens that shows a zoomed-in view while you tap, so you can place annotations precisely on any smartphone without needing a stylus or external keyboard.

Your annotations are permanent

Annotations are embedded directly into the PDF using pdf-lib. They are not a separate annotation layer — they become part of the page content. The downloaded PDF looks identical in every PDF reader and cannot be stripped of its annotations.

Frequently asked questions

What annotation tools are available?

Text labels, arrows, rectangles, check marks (✓), crosses (✗), plus (+) and minus (−) symbols. All annotations are permanently embedded into the downloaded PDF.

Can I annotate a PDF on my phone?

Yes. PDFree works on iOS and Android browsers. The built-in zoom slider and magnifier make precise placement easy on small screens.

Are my annotations saved if I close the browser?

Annotations exist only in the current browser session. Download your annotated PDF before closing the tab.

Do I need to install any software or extension?

No installation needed. PDFree runs entirely in your browser — no app, no plugin, no extension required.
