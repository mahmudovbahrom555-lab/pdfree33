Document Scanner — Turn Photos into a Clean PDF

Need to scan a contract, receipt, ID, or handwritten note but don't have a scanner? PDFree's Document Scanner turns your phone or webcam into one — take a photo (or use an existing one from your gallery), and the tool automatically finds the document's edges, straightens out any angle, and cleans it up for you.

Unlike PDFree's JPG to PDF tool — built for quickly combining photos you already have exactly as they are — every photo added here goes through the same crop-and-straighten review, camera or gallery, because that's the whole point of a scanner: turning an imperfect photo of a page into something that reads like it came off an actual scanner.

How it works

  1. Take a photo or pick one from your gallery. The camera view works right in your browser — no app to install.
  2. Review the auto-detected crop. Drag any of the four corners if the automatic edge detection needs a nudge, or choose Skip if the photo doesn't need cropping at all.
  3. Choose Color or Grayscale, add more pages the same way, then save the whole thing as one PDF.

Why it's different from a phone's built-in scanner app

Nothing leaves your device. The photo, the edge detection, the perspective correction — it all happens locally in your browser, the same way every PDFree tool works. There's no account, no app download, and no file size limit.
