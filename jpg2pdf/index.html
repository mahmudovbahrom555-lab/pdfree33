JPG to PDF Converter — Free, Unlimited, No Upload

Turn your photos, scanned documents, or screenshots into professional PDF files. PDFree automatically detects image orientation from EXIF data and converts JPG, JPEG, PNG files to PDF — all in your browser with zero uploads.

Features

  • Auto Rotation: Reads EXIF orientation data to display photos correctly
  • Multiple Page Sizes: A4, Letter, or fit-to-image (no white borders)
  • Portrait / Landscape: Choose page orientation or let the tool decide automatically
  • Optional Compression: Reduce PDF size by recompressing images (adjustable quality)
  • Batch Conversion: Convert multiple images into a single multi-page PDF

Common Use Cases

Scanned Documents: Convert phone photos of receipts, forms, or handwritten notes into archival PDFs.

Photography Portfolios: Package multiple images into a single PDF for client review or printing.

Digital Signatures: Convert a scanned signature image to PDF for inserting into contracts.

Presentations: Turn infographic images or slides into PDF format for distribution.

⚡ Pro Tip: Enable "Compress images" and set quality to 85% for a good balance between file size and visual clarity. Perfect for emailing or web publishing.

Privacy & Technical Details

Your images never leave your device. PDFree uses the HTML5 Canvas API to read pixel data from your JPG files, then embeds them into a new PDF document using pdf-lib. The entire process happens in your browser's JavaScript engine — no server sees your photos.

EXIF orientation handling ensures photos taken on phones display correctly even if the camera rotated the image. This automatic correction is often missing from other converters, leading to sideways PDFs.
