When You Need to Split a PDF Into Separate Files

📋 Share only part of a document A 60-page report with a 5-page executive summary. Extract those 5 pages and share without the rest.
📧 Reduce file size for email A 40 MB combined scan that's too large to email. Split into 4 × 10 MB files and send in parts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I split a PDF into individual pages?

Yes. Use the "All pages" option to split every page into its own PDF. Files download as a ZIP archive, named page-1.pdf, page-2.pdf, and so on.

What page range format does PDFree accept?

Enter comma-separated pages and ranges: 1, 3-7, 12, 15-20. Spaces are optional. You can also click individual thumbnails to select pages visually.

What is the difference between Split and Extract?

Split lets you choose pages and download them as multiple separate files or as one combined PDF. Extract is simpler — it pulls a single page range into one new PDF. Use Split when you need multiple output files.

Is there a page limit when splitting?

No server-side limit. The practical limit is your browser's RAM. PDFs with hundreds of pages and high-resolution images may take a few seconds to render thumbnails.

Is my document private when I split it?

Completely. PDFree splits files using JavaScript in your browser. No data is transmitted to any server — your document never leaves your device.
